# Digital Civilization Engine

## 1. Overview

The Digital Civilization Engine provides continuous, objective assessment of ecosystem health, collective productivity, knowledge velocity, and systemic opportunity discovery across the entire planetary network.

## 2. Health Metrics & Scoring Model

The civilization health score is derived from six weighted indexes:
1. **Innovation Index** ($W = 0.20$): Rate of patent filings, open-source breakthroughs, and benchmark advancements.
2. **Knowledge Growth Index** ($W = 0.20$): Verified research publications, synthesis models, and inter-university collaborations.
3. **Economic Activity Index** ($W = 0.20$): Volume of cross-border autonomous compute contracts and bounty settlements.
4. **Workforce Readiness Index** ($W = 0.20$): Developer certification velocity, skill adaptation rate, and automated role matching.
5. **Research Productivity Index** ($W = 0.10$): Formal proof verification rate and collaborative academic output.
6. **Governance & Resilience Index** ($W = 0.10$): Automated policy compliance, low security incident latency, and zero-trust verification.

### Civilization Health Tiers
- **Pristine** ($Score \ge 95.0$): Zero systemic bottlenecks; frictionless global talent and research flow.
- **Advancing** ($85.0 \le Score < 95.0$): Accelerating growth across key innovation corridors.
- **Stable** ($70.0 \le Score < 85.0$): Steady equilibrium with minor compute or talent imbalances.
- **Degrading** ($50.0 \le Score < 70.0$): Regional knowledge silos or compute liquidity constraints.
- **Critical** ($Score < 50.0$): Immediate governance intervention required.

## 3. Automated Civilization Reports
The system periodically aggregates metrics and generates detailed strategic forecasts, risk heatmaps, and high-impact interventions to optimize global compute allocation.
