# 04-Academic Knowledge Graph & Publishing Engine

This document outlines the indexing of theorems and publication citation tracking.

## Knowledge Graph Civilization

- **Knowledge Nodes**: Map concepts, skills, projects, companies, certifications, research papers, theories, algorithms, datasets, benchmarks, and theorems.
- **Derivation Lineage**: Tracks mathematical proof derivation steps (e.g. Noetherian constraint -> manifold optimization -> tensor renormalization).

## Publishing Workspace

- Drafts manuscript full Markdown documents with automatic, isolated DOI registry allocation.
- Tracks citation counts and citation velocity to calculate impact factors.
