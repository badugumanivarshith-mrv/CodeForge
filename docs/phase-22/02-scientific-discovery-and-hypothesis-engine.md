# 02-Scientific Discovery & Hypothesis Engine

The **Scientific Discovery & Hypothesis Engine** handles formulation, testing, and confirmation of candidate academic theories.

## Core Operations

1. **Formulate Hypothesis**:
   - Accepts statement and epistemic rationale.
   - Computes a novelty score (based on existing knowledge nodes overlap) and feasibility score (based on department resources).
   - Generates a concrete test plan with simulation parameters.
2. **Confirm Discovery**:
   - Assesses empirical evidence.
   - Assigns a significance tier (e.g. paradigm-shifting, breakthrough).
   - Evaluates reproducibility across multiple runs.
