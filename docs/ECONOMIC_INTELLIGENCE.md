# Global Economic Intelligence

## 1. Macroeconomic Signal Ingestion

Global Economic Intelligence monitors real-time market dynamics across the planetary developer and compute economy.

### Signal Types
- `COMPUTE_DEMAND`: GPU/TPU cluster demand spikes and token throughput pricing.
- `TALENT_INFLOW`: Cross-border developer migration, role transitions, and talent pool depth.
- `CAPITAL_FLOW`: Venture financing rounds, institutional grants, and tokenized project funding.
- `SKILL_PREMIUM`: Premium compensation rates for emerging competencies (e.g., formal logic, neuromorphic computing).
- `MARKET_VOLATILITY`: Fluctuations in compute liquidity and project completion timelines.
- `INNOVATION_VELOCITY`: Acceleration in patent filings and open-source releases.

## 2. Macroeconomic Forecasting Engine
Simulates 3, 6, 12, and 24-month horizon macroeconomic trajectories. Generates predictive talent demand growth curves, skill premium trend heatmaps, and regional investment recommendations.
