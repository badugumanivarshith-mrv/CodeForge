# AI Research Civilization

## 1. Multi-Institution Academic Federations

The AI Research Civilization connects leading academic departments, research labs, and enterprise R&D swarms into collaborative federations.

## 2. Cryptographic Proof Validation
Collaborations are secured with zero-knowledge cryptographic proofs (`validationProof` hash), enabling decentralized peer review, reproducible benchmark validation, and instant credit distribution.

## 3. Key Capabilities
- **Federated Lab Consortiums**: Shared compute clusters across universities for large-scale model training.
- **Autonomous Peer Review**: Multi-agent reviewer panels providing automated sanity checking, counterexample generation, and formal verification.
- **Planetary Citation & Impact Graph**: Real-time tracking of foundational discovery lineage and applied technology transfer.
