# Global Innovation Network

## 1. Breakthrough Discovery Engine

The Global Innovation Network indexes and verifies breakthrough technologies across enterprises, university laboratories, and independent research swarms.

## 2. Technology Domains
- **AI Reasoning**: LLM theorem proving, multi-step neuro-symbolic reasoning, and autonomous code synthesis kernels.
- **Quantum Compute**: Quantum key distribution, error-mitigated circuit synthesis, and post-quantum cryptography.
- **Biotech & Synthetic Biology**: Protein design architectures, metabolic pathway modeling, and in-silico drug discovery.
- **CleanTech**: Grid-scale battery chemistry, fusion energy magnetics, and smart microgrid dispatchers.
- **Autonomous Systems**: Decentralized swarm robotics, self-healing data centers, and multi-agent coordination.
- **Cybersecurity**: Lattice-based signature schemes, verified micro-kernels, and Zero-Trust identity enclaves.

## 3. Commercial Readiness & Ranking System
Innovations are evaluated using Technology Maturity Levels (TML 1-9), adoption forecast velocities, and commercial readiness scores. Domain rankings highlight the most impactful breakthroughs ready for ecosystem commercialization.
