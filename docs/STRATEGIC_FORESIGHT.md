The Strategic Foresight Engine delivers long-term anticipatory intelligence across multiple planning horizons:
- **1-Year Horizon (Tactical)**: Immediate skill demand shifts, emerging framework adoption, and compute bottlenecks.
- **3-Year Horizon (Strategic)**: Multi-agent enterprise workflows, enterprise migration patterns, and automated DevOps dominance.
- **5-Year Horizon (Transformational)**: Autonomous digital organizations, ubiquitous formal verification, and post-silicon compute transition.
- **10-Year Horizon (Civilizational)**: Universal machine intelligence interfaces, planetary energy-compute balance, and autonomous scientific synthesis.
- **25-Year Horizon (Long-Range Speculative)**: Interstellar compute meshes, post-scarcity knowledge infrastructures, and cognitive digital twins.
