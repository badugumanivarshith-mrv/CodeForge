# Predictive Intelligence Engine Documentation

## 1. Multi-Horizon Forecasting Engine

The Predictive Intelligence Engine delivers Bayesian probability forecasts across six distinct horizons:
- `SEVEN_DAYS`: Immediate sprint task completion and cognitive load trends.
- `THIRTY_DAYS`: Monthly skill mastery progression and project milestones.
- `NINETY_DAYS`: Quarterly career advancement and multi-agent workload distributions.
- `ONE_YEAR`: Annual architectural scaling and technology adoption shifts.
- `THREE_YEARS`: Industry competence demand shifts and autonomous workflow dominance.
- `FIVE_YEARS`: Long-range civilizational intelligence growth and compute transition trajectories.

## 2. Risk Modeling & Recommendations
Each forecast generates:
- **Success Probability** ($0.0 - 1.0$)
- **Expected Outcomes** with metrics, trends, and projected values
- **Identified Risk Factors**
- **Actionable Interventions** for preemptive trajectory steering
